# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Inferred from the request for a static website: plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with no framework or build step.

## Users

Inferred from the brief: Portland visitors and repeat visitors choosing coffee shops and doughnut shops for themselves or visiting friends.

## Product Purpose

Help visitors quickly choose where to go by curating six Portland coffee spots and six doughnut spots, with an interactive map and useful place details.

## Positioning

An opinionated, visitor-friendly shortlist that pairs local recommendations with a map, rather than an exhaustive directory.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The site is a static, client-side guide. Business hours and offerings can change; visitors should be able to verify current details with the businesses before heading out.

## Evidence on Hand

The user supplied the audience, purpose, categories, shortlist size, map interaction, and preference for a visually rich site. No first-party business data or visual assets were supplied; recommendations and place details will be checked against public sources and linked to current business information where available.

## Product Principles

- Make the shortlist useful to someone unfamiliar with Portland.
- Let visitors move between a place and its map location.
- Keep the guide fast, responsive, and usable without an account.
- Treat recommendations as editorial picks, not objective rankings.
- Make changing business details easy to verify.
